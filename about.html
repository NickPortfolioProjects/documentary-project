About D‑Cine

D‑Cine is a digital home built for documentary lovers, creators, and curious minds. Whether you're a filmmaker exploring new modes of storytelling or a viewer searching for your next deep dive, D‑Cine brings the world of non‑fiction cinema together in one place.

Our mission is simple: **celebrate real stories**. From investigative journalism to poetic visual essays, from micro‑genres to experimental modes, D‑Cine helps you discover, learn, and connect with the global documentary landscape.

Our Story

D‑Cine began as a passion project created by filmmakers who wanted a space dedicated entirely to documentary culture. No algorithms pushing blockbusters. No noise. Just real stories, real creators, and real impact.

Today, D‑Cine curates a growing library of documentaries, micro‑genres, modes, and creator profiles — all searchable, all organised, all designed to help you explore the craft of non‑fiction cinema.

What We Offer

  • A curated database of documentaries from around the world
  • Genre, micro‑genre, and documentary mode taxonomy
  • Creator profiles and project showcases
  • Tools for discovery, research, and inspiration
  • A growing community of storytellers and enthusiasts

Whether you're researching, watching, or creating — **D‑Cine is your gateway into the world of documentary filmmaking.**
